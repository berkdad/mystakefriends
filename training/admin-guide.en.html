My Stake Friends — Administrator Guide

"Hearts Knit in Unity"
❤
⚠ The golden rule: ALL administration happens on the website — mystakefriends.com. The mobile app is only for members participating in circles. Signing in to the app will not show any admin tools; in the app, you are simply a member of your own circle.
1

Sign in on the website

Go to mystakefriends.com on any computer or tablet and sign in with your admin email and password. Use the English | Español switch on the login card to change language at any time.

2

Know your dashboard

Everything lives in two controls at the top:

  • View Ward — switch between all wards or focus on one. Most actions apply to the ward you are viewing.
  • Actions menu — admins, email, push notifications, announcements, and your own member view.

The tiles switch between Friends (your member list), Circles (the circle builder), Reports, and Drop In.

3

Add your members

  • Upload Roster — import an LCR export (Excel/CSV). Map the columns, choose whether the roster is Female or Male members, and import.
  • Add Member — add one person quickly. Every member needs their own email address — spouses must not share one.
  • Invite All — emails everyone who has never logged in. The invite has them set a password on the website first, then download the app.
  • The bell badge on each card shows whether that member's phone has notifications on or off.

4

Build friendship circles

  • Open Circles with a single ward selected, then Create Circle.
  • Drag members from Available Members into circles; drag back to remove.
  • Click the crown on a member to make them circle captain.
  • Use filters (age, marital status, has email) to plan balanced circles.
5

Go Live when you're ready

Circles start in Edit Mode — invisible to members, so you can rearrange freely. Clicking Go Live shows exactly who will be notified (push + email) before you confirm. You can switch back to Edit Mode anytime; removed members are never notified.

6

Communicate

  • Announcements — a banner members see when opening the app; set how long it shows, optionally with a push.
  • Notify — instant push notification to the ward or stake.
  • Email — email a circle, a ward, the stake, or all admins.
7

Drop In

Visit any circle's chat and activities temporarily to say hello — one circle at a time. Anything you post stays after you leave, labeled with your name.

Troubleshooting

  • "I can't log in to the app" — they must first open their invitation email and set a password on the website. No invite? Send one from their member card.
  • App says "Not assigned to a circle yet" — the app only works for members placed in a Live circle. Add them to a circle and go Live.
  • "I don't get notifications" — check the bell badge on their card. Off means their phone is blocking them: in the app they'll see a banner with an Open Settings button. "No report" means they need the latest app version.
  • Password reset — open the member's card → Reset Password sets a new one instantly (no email is sent — tell them the new password).
  • Couples sharing one email — each spouse needs their own email address, or their accounts will collide.
