"Hearts Knit in Unity" — Member Quick Start
❤
⚠ Read this first: the mobile app only works after two things happen: (1) you set your password on the website using your invitation email, and (2) your ward leader places you in a friendship circle. If you open the app before that, it won't have anything to show you yet.
1

Open your invitation email

You'll receive an email titled "Welcome to My Stake Friends!" from your ward leadership. Click the Set Your Password button inside it. (Check spam or junk if you don't see it.)

2  Set your password on the website

The button opens the page shown here. Choose a password (at least 8 characters, with uppercase, lowercase, and a number). This step must happen before the app will work.

3  Download the app and sign in

 App Store▶ Google Play
Search for "My Stake Friends" and install it. Sign in with your email and the password you just created. When the app asks to send notifications, tap Allow.

"Not assigned to a circle yet"?

That's normal! It means your ward leader hasn't placed you in a friendship circle yet, or your circle isn't live. The app is built entirely around your circle, so there's nothing to see outside it. Contact your ward leadership and check back soon.

4

Meet your circle

Once you're in a live circle, your dashboard shows everything: your circle members, group chat, upcoming activities, and your profile. Fill out your profile (hobbies, interests, a photo) so your circle can get to know you!

5

Notifications and language

Tap the ⓘ icon at the top of your dashboard to see whether notifications are on, and to switch to Español. Your language choice follows you everywhere: app, website, and emails.

If notifications are off, you'll see a yellow banner. Tap Open Settings, then Notifications, then turn on Allow Notifications. Without this you won't hear about new messages from your circle.

Good to know

  • The website (mystakefriends.com) has your circle too. Sign in with the same email and password.
  • Forgot your password? Tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen.
